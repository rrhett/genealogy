## Biographical Summary
Mary West Rainwater, daughter of Thomas and Sarah Miller West and wife of Thomas Rainwater, received the west 60 acres of the family's French Broad River tract in 1882 (`[S-1882-LAND-WEST-FRENCH-BROAD]`). Her daughter Octa Rainwater later married Mary's nephew Claude West in 1908, reuniting the two halves of the 120-acre tract.
