## Biographical Summary
Claude West was born 14 May 1886 in Cocke County, Tennessee, son of John West (`[S-1900-CENSUS-WEST-COCKE-TN]`, `[S-1954-DEATH-WEST-CLAUDE]`). He married Octa Rainwater in Cocke County on 15 March 1908 (`[S-1908-MARRIAGE-WEST-RAINWATER]`) and farmed in Civil District 4 near Newport (`[S-1910-CENSUS-WEST-COCKE-TN]`, `[S-1917-MILITARY-WEST-CLAUDE]`). He died in Newport on 12 November 1954 and was buried at Union Cemetery (`[S-1954-DEATH-WEST-CLAUDE]`).

## Recorded Discrepancies
- **Birth Year (1886 vs. 1887):** The 1900 Census (May 1886, age 14), 1910 Census (age 23 in April 1910), and 1954 Death Certificate (14 May 1886) all align with **May 1886**. His self-signed WWI Draft Registration Card (`[S-1917-MILITARY-WEST-CLAUDE]`) records **14 May 1887** (age 30), a common 1-year self-reporting discrepancy; 1886 is preferred due to the contemporaneous 1900 enumeration.
- **Given Name Spelling:** Recorded as "Claud West" on his 1908 marriage register entry (`[S-1908-MARRIAGE-WEST-RAINWATER]`) and "Claude West" elsewhere.
