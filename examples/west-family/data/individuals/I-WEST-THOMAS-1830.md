Thomas West (born about 1830) and his wife Sarah Miller West partitioned their 120-acre French Broad River farm in Civil District 4, Cocke County, Tennessee, between their children John West and Mary West Rainwater in October 1882 (`[S-1882-LAND-WEST-FRENCH-BROAD]`).
