## Formal Citation
Cocke County, Tennessee, Deed Book 14, p. 208, Thomas West and wife Sarah Miller West to John West and Mary West Rainwater, 12 October 1882; Register of Deeds, Newport.

## Verbatim Transcription & Extracted Data
- **Grantors:** Thomas West (b. abt 1830) and wife Sarah Miller West (b. abt 1834), Civil District 4
- **Tract:** 120 acres on the south bank of the French Broad River, Civil District 4, Cocke County, Tennessee
- **Conveyance:** East 60 acres to son John West; West 60 acres to daughter Mary West Rainwater (wife of Thomas Rainwater), to descend to her children.
- **Witnesses:** J. M. Stokely, W. H. Blazer
