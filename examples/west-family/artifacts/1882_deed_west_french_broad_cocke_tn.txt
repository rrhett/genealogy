Cocke County, Tennessee Deed Book 14, p. 208 — Recorded 12 October 1882
Grantors: Thomas West (b. abt 1830) and wife Sarah Miller West (b. abt 1834), Civil District 4
Tract: 120 acres on the south bank of the French Broad River, Civil District 4, Cocke County, Tennessee
Partition / Conveyance:
  - East 60 acres conveyed to son John West
  - West 60 acres conveyed to daughter Mary West Rainwater (wife of Thomas Rainwater), to descend to her children
Witnesses: J. M. Stokely, W. H. Blazer
